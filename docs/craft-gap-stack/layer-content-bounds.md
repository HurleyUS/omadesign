# Painted bounds for merged and stamped layers

Merged and stamped layers now retain the smallest integer pixel rectangle containing nonzero rendered alpha. Geometric padding and transparent canvas are removed after rendering, so Move/Free Transform/alignment work on the painted content. The layer origin shifts by the removed rows/columns; masks, clipped stacks, soft edges, outer effects and off-canvas content are rendered first. A nonzero float alpha remains part of the bounds even when its display-cache alpha rounds to zero. No alpha threshold, resampling or color conversion is added.

The same admitted compositor result supplies exact copied cache/RGBA/CMYKA rows. Depth, intent and shared ICC bytes remain unchanged. Fully covered images retain their allocations. Empty paint becomes one transparent pixel at the document origin. Opaque Flatten keeps its full canvas; it is not cropped. Existing captured workers, cancellation, stale guards, typed history and native reopen remain authoritative. Existing resource/API/model/schema/shortcut/render math paths are unchanged.
