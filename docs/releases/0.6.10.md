# Omadesign 0.6.10 — Effects in every color space

0.6.10 follows [0.6.9](0.6.9.md). It fixes a color bug in 0.6.9's effect rendering and finishes the export and placement work.

## Fixed

- **Wide-gamut content with effects:** 0.6.9 rendered every layer with effects through 8-bit sRGB in 16-bit, float or profile-tagged documents. Colors outside sRGB were clipped and precision was lost. Shadows, glows, inner shadows and color overlays are now built from the layer's alpha and converted into the document profile, and the layer's own samples are kept unchanged.
- **Group and clipping effects:** effects on groups and on the base of a clipping stack in 16-bit, float, tagged or CMYK documents render instead of refusing the export.
- **CMYK effect colors:** in CMYK documents, effect colors are converted through the document's CMYK profile into each ink. The layer's inks are unchanged.
- **Other refusals in precise export:** SVG filter graphs, live artwork masks and vector content with effects or patterns now render instead of refusing.

## Changed

- **Placing images:** placed and imported artwork is converted to the document's color model, profile **and depth**. A 16-bit or float photo placed in an 8-bit document becomes 8-bit; a photo placed in a 16-bit document becomes 16-bit.
- **Export formats:**
  - JPEG from 16-bit, float or profile-tagged RGB documents uses the document's colors and embeds its profile. JPEG is 8 bits per channel.
  - PNG from float documents is 16 bits per channel. Only TIFF, PSD and PSB keep float samples.
  - PNG and JPEG from CMYK documents convert the ink composite through the document profile to sRGB.
  - SVG, PDF, ORA, IDML and AI upscaling use a temporary 8-bit sRGB copy. Each image is converted from its own profile.
  - Export never changes the document.

## Known limitations

- **Pixel filters are 8-bit:** blur, color adjustments (saturation, hue, brightness, contrast, invert, color matrix) and effect stacks from older projects run through the 8-bit effect engine in the document's color space. On a 16-bit or float layer they reduce that layer to 8-bit precision, and float values below 0 or above 1 (HDR) are clipped. Arbitrary HDR content is not preserved through these filters.
- **CMYK color adjustments:** in CMYK documents, filters run on each ink separately. Blur and offset match; saturation, hue, brightness, contrast, invert and color-matrix adjustments do not match adjusting the color.
- **Raster layers with SVG filter graphs or live artwork masks** in 16-bit, float, tagged or CMYK documents are drawn through 8-bit sRGB for that layer.
- **Writers without profiles:** SVG, PDF, ORA, IDML and AI upscaling write 8-bit sRGB. Colors outside sRGB are clipped and extra precision is lost. Export TIFF, PSD or PSB to keep a document's full color and depth.
