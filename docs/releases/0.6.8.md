# Omadesign 0.6.8 — Craft parity

0.6.8 is the first stable release since 0.6.3. It ships the 0.6.4–0.6.7 milestones and the 0.6.8 Craft parity stack (PRs #220–#451) in a single release.

## 0.6.8: Craft parity

- **Native float compositor:** conic gradients and shape gradients (#450, #451).
- **Color:** Gray and Lab tagged documents. Conversion happens at display, export, PSD import and in the picker. Documents saved with these tags use **project file format version 40** (#449). Recolor adds reduction presets (#444).
- **Menus with real commands:** Motion Animation, Layout, Develop and Effect menus, plus layer effects from the Effect menu (#426–#429, #445).
- **Motion, Photo and Layout:** reverse and clear animation (#446), auto white balance (#447), and Remove Auto Layout (#448). The Develop panel's Auto light is a single undo step (#431).
- **Channels and selections:** the active pixel selection is saved with the document (#432). Saved channels and the selection flip and rotate exactly (#433). Layer masks run through the shared scalar filter engine (#434). Spot ink channels have a canvas overlay (#435), alpha and spot channels exchange with Photoshop files (#436), and channel Calculations are available (#437).
- **Asset Export:** bleed, JPEG quality, PNG-8/WebP/GIF variants and row reordering (#438). Layers can be dragged from the Layers panel into the export pane (#439).
- **Symbols, styles and actions:** replace symbol instances in place (#440), import symbol libraries (#441), linked document graphic styles (#442), and move/align action steps with action file exchange (#443).
- **Layers:** grouping, group-tree rows and whole-subtree Arrange. Duplicate, merge, flatten, clipping, stamp and cross-group merges, with paint-accurate bounds and selection restored on Undo/Redo (#406–#425).
- **Type:** Find Font, Find and Replace, editable variable font instances, and font embedding in PDF and SVG. Editable SVG text keeps metrics, kerning, scale, bidi, fallback and leading (#368–#404).
- **Pixel:** alpha channels, Quick Mask, channel Curves and filters, and Pixel adjustments and filters in the top menus (#356–#368).

## 0.6.4–0.6.7

- **0.6.4:** qualified baseline. Affine focal radial SVG gradients and editable linear optical corrections in Photo.
- **0.6.5:** shared inspector and panel primitives.
- **0.6.6:** contextual mixed and locked fields. Vector PDF gradients, masks and transparency are preserved. Styled and threaded IDML exchange is connected to Open and Export.
- **0.6.7:** saved task panels with reliable restore order. Native 16-bit/float pixels, CMYK and ICC color, and editable Photoshop type and placed sources. Live collaboration with presence and per-author undo. Motion expressions, compositions and 3D scenes. SVG filters and patterns. Editable PDF text with embedded fonts. Affinity and Illustrator adapters. Broad keyboard access across native fields. See the [0.6.7 nightly notes](0.6.7-nightly.20261016.0001.md).

## Known limitations

The limits in the 0.6.7 nightly notes still apply. Full float SVG filters, patterns and appearance isolation refuse explicitly. Tablet behavior, physical display accuracy, Adobe/InDesign compatibility and every RAW camera mode are not claimed as qualified.
