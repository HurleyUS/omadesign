# Omadesign 0.6.9 — Export what you placed

0.6.9 is a fix release on top of [0.6.8 Craft parity](0.6.8.md).

## Fixed

- **Export with placed photos and effects:** in 0.6.8, a document with ICC-tagged placed photos (for example camera JPEGs) and a layer effect such as Drop Shadow failed every export with "Apply pixel effects before high-depth export". PNG, JPEG, TIFF, SVG, PDF, PSD, PSB, ORA and IDML exports now work, from the app and from `omadesign --convert`. Existing documents export as they are; export never changes the document, its layers or objects.
- **Effects in precise exports:** the native float compositor now renders raster layer effects through the effect engine instead of refusing. Only that layer's effects are rendered at 8 bits; other samples keep their precision.

## Changed

- **Placing images:** placed and imported artwork is converted to the document's color model and profile. Samples deeper than the document keep their precision. An 8-bit tagged photo placed in an untagged 8-bit RGB document becomes ordinary 8-bit RGB.

## Known limitations

Group effects, clipping-base effects, SVG filter graphs and live artwork masks in 16-bit, float or CMYK documents still refuse precise export with an explicit message. JPEG, PDF, SVG, ORA and IDML still refuse documents whose own color is 16-bit, float, CMYK or tagged; export PNG, TIFF or PSD for those.
