# Brush packs

Paint with 12 original Omadesign tips. Essentials covers Round, Soft, Flat,
Calligraphy, Pencil and Dry ink. Texture adds Chalk, Charcoal, Grain, Stipple,
Wash and Spatter. They're CC0: yours to use, change and share.

[Download the packs and current brush nightly](/brushes).

In Pixel, open **Brush → Import**. Choose an Omadesign pack, Photoshop ABR,
Illustrator library, PNG or SVG. Select a tip, adjust its settings, then paint.
Save a preset or export your own pack. Imported packs stay on your computer.

Adobe artwork becomes sampled tips. Photoshop dynamics and editable Illustrator
brush behavior are not reproduced. Your original files stay intact.
